# Devil Fruits — Block Pirate

Every fruit in the game: rarity, in-game item, description and **all** of its abilities
with their cooldowns. Every fruit sits in its own code block: copy one and paste it
straight into Discord.

> 41 fruits · 181 abilities in total · 152 different abilities.

---

## How to get a fruit

- **The fruit machine**: costs `500 + 35 × level`. A `Fruit Voucher` takes 10 to 50% off.
- **Wild fruits**: a fruit block spawns on the islands (18 spawn points), click it.
- **Loot**: mobs and bosses drop items on the ground (fruit vouchers, Haki
  Fragments), and bosses keep their own reserved drops.
- A fruit is **kept on death** and can be **dropped** to hand it to another player.
  You can only hold one fruit: eating a new one replaces the old one.

## How to cast an ability

- **Mobile**: every ability is an **item** in your inventory. Hold it and tap. The dash
  has its own on-screen button.
- **PC (items, default)**: same thing, one item per ability, left click to cast.
- **PC (buttons)**: hold `Fruit Abilities` and use `Z X C V F` for the abilities in the
  order listed below. Dash is on `Q`.
- Each ability has its own **cooldown**: the delay before you can cast it again.
